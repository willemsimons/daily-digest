For you

FILM
Sans Soleil
Chris Marker

A film that operates as an essay on memory, ritual, and how cameras construct meaning — Marker wanders between Tokyo's vending machine shrines and Guinean funeral rites, treating footage as raw material for a theory of human consciousness. It will make you want to rebuild how you think about images entirely.

ART
Lee Bul — The Anatomy of Every Setting Sun
Lee Bul

Lee Bul's work sits exactly at the edge you care about: cyborg bodies, utopian architecture collapsing into ruin, sci-fi mythology built from obsessive physical craft. Her suspended installation work in particular makes visible the invisible feedback loop between aspiration and entropy.

MUSIC
Drexciya discography (starting with The Quest)
Drexciya

A mythology built entirely in sound: James Stinson and Gerald Donald constructed an entire underwater civilization of the descendants of enslaved Africans thrown overboard during the Middle Passage, told through machine-perfect electro. It's world-building as resistance, identity, and systems design — with zero interviews or public appearances to explain it.

Happening in New York

2026 Whitney Biennial (closing weeks)
Whitney Museum of American Art, 99 Gansevoort St, Manhattan · Aug 17–23

Last call on the 82nd Biennial, curated by Marcela Guerrero and Drew Sawyer — one of the three large NYC survey shows closing this month, and the one most likely to have contemporary artists working at the physical/digital and identity edge you respond to.

Lily van der Stokker — Male Art Exhibition, Great Show
Kaufmann Repetto, 55 Walker Street, TriBeCa · Aug 17–28

Van der Stokker makes work that looks decorative and cheerful on the surface and is structurally corrosive underneath — her paintings interrogate gender, institutional power, and what counts as 'serious' art with a deadpan playfulness that earns it.

Acne Studios × Frédéric Malle Immersive Installation
Éditions de Parfums Frédéric Malle, 120 Prince Street, SoHo · Aug 17–26

The Prince Street boutique has been transformed into an immersive Acne-pink bathroom environment — it's a case study in how two design-obsessed houses translate brand ethos into multisensory space, which is more interesting as an artifact of design thinking than as a shopping experience.

Dopplereffekt + DJ Stingray + bookworms
314 Scholes Street, Bushwick, Brooklyn · Fri, Aug 21

Dopplereffekt (Gerald Donald, the Drexciyan half who never plays live) alongside DJ Stingray in a raw Bushwick industrial space — machine music from one of electronic music's most hermetic mythologists, in the exact kind of environment where the social system is visible before it hardens.

Rival Consoles + Arushi Jain
The Hall at Elsewhere, 599 Johnson Ave, Brooklyn · Thu, Aug 27

Rival Consoles makes meticulously structured electronic music where every patch and decay feels considered; Arushi Jain bridges North Indian classical vocals with modular synthesis — together it's a rare double bill where both acts are doing something genuinely their own.
