For you

FILM
Sans Soleil
Chris Marker

A film-essay that works like a generative system — images, memory, and ritual looping through Tokyo, Guinea-Bissau, and Iceland, assembling a theory of how cultures dream. The invisible architecture of collective consciousness made visible in 100 minutes; it will make you want to build something.

MUSIC
Pomegranates
Nicolas Jaar

A 2015 record structured like a cosmology — nine tracks that feel like field recordings from an invented civilization, each one a closed ritual with its own internal logic. The kind of album that clarifies what you're trying to do when you're in the middle of building something.

ART
UUmwelt
Pierre Huyghe

An AI-generated mental-image machine — Huyghe trained a system on people imagining things and then had it hallucinate those images in a continuous loop on screens. It's a feedback loop between mind and representation made physical; the boundary between biology, technology, and environment dissolves in real time.
