Pax Silica — The US Government's Named Framework for AI as Foreign Policy
U.S. Department of State

The State Department has given a name and a doctrine to what was previously implicit: 'Pax Silica' is the US framework for treating compute, critical minerals, and AI supply chains as the geopolitical equivalent of 20th-century oil and steel. Declaration signatories commit to aligned AI infrastructure — meaning non-signatories are, by design, outside the consensus. Worth reading as a primary source because the framing is unusually candid about what's actually being organized.

DHS 'Predictive Policing' Units Are Analyzing Americans' Financial Habits — Then Pulling Them Over
404 Media

Border Patrol's Predictive Intelligence Targeting Teams (PITT) analyze financial behavior and feed intelligence to local police, who then execute stops under pretextual violations — in one case, an 'obstructed license plate' in Montana after the system flagged a man's financial activity. The CDT's framing is precise: 'genuine probable cause cannot be synthetically generated,' and when the underlying model is secret, there's no meaningful way to challenge a stop. This is parallel construction industrialized.

Systems that shape what survives

There's a New 'Google Jail' for Independent Wikis
Weird Gloop

Since a March 2024 algorithm change, roughly 90% of wikis launching on brand-new domains show up in Google Search for only their main page — catastrophic for knowledge communities whose traffic is almost entirely search-driven on article pages. The practical result is that independent wikis migrating off Fandom (which is covered in auto-playing video ads) can't escape into the open web without effectively disappearing. One infrastructure player's ranking logic is quietly deciding which community knowledge projects are viable.

LibreOffice Breaks Download Records After Declaring It Has No AI Features
Manual do Usuário

LibreOffice 26.8 hit over a million downloads in its first week — a record — after The Document Foundation explicitly stated it won't ship generative AI by default until the technology meets six principles including zero telemetry, no content leaving the device without authorization, and no single-vendor dependency. The foundation's framing: 'Yes, no AI is now a feature.' Absence of a capability becoming the product signal is worth tracking.

Science at the edge of translation

WeatherNext 3 — DeepMind's Hourly Global Forecast Model, Landing as NOAA Gets Defunded
Google DeepMind

DeepMind's third-generation weather model generates hourly forecasts by drawing directly from raw satellite imagery rather than preprocessed data, hitting 5km resolution on surface temperature and humidity — significantly more accurate than prior models even in locations not seen during training. The timing is the real story: this capability is expanding precisely as public forecasting infrastructure in the US is being gutted. Private AI weather capability and defunded public meteorology are now on diverging trajectories.

Off the map

The Two Christian Saints Who Are Secretly the Buddha
Signore Galilei

Saints Barlaam and Josaphat — venerated in both Catholic and Orthodox calendars — are a direct structural retelling of the Buddha's life story, transmitted via a Persian intermediary ('Bodisav' from 'Bodhisattva'), then into Arabic as 'Budhasaf', then into Georgian as the Balavariani in the 10th century, and finally absorbed into Christian hagiography so completely that the origin was invisible for centuries. The name 'Josaphat' is a phonetic corruption of 'Bodhisattva.' This is not influence or borrowing — it is wholesale identity laundering across three civilizations.
