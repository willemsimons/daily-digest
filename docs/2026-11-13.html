For you

FILM
Sans Soleil
Chris Marker

A meditation on memory, ritual, and the hidden systems by which cultures encode meaning into images — Marker treats documentary footage like a generative system, finding the deep structure under the surface of everyday life. Made in 1983, still the sharpest film about how images construct reality.

MUSIC
Reassemblage
Visible Cloaks

Spencer Doran and Ryan Carlile build music that sits exactly at the edge where physical and digital meet — acoustic instruments processed into something that feels like watching light refract through glass, beautiful on the surface with a rigorous generative architecture underneath.

ART
The Paintings for the Temple
Hilma af Klint

A hidden system of symbols, sacred geometry, and cosmological feedback loops that af Klint worked on in obsessive secrecy for years — art that is literally a diagram of invisible forces, and one of the great examples of someone going unreasonably deep on one narrow thing.

Happening in New York

Elmgreen & Dragset: TRANSITION
Pace Gallery, 508 & 510 West 25th Street, Chelsea · Nov 6 – Jan 16

A 40-ton traversable corten-steel Oval Office you can walk through — architecture as power critique, physical environment as psychological experience; exactly the kind of obsessive, category-blurring installation that uses built space to make a system visible.

Gottlieb/Rothko: The Surrealist Years
Pace Gallery, 540 West 25th Street, Chelsea · Nov 13 – Jan 9

A focused look at the moment two painters were actively constructing a new symbolic language — before Abstract Expressionism hardened into an institution, when the system was still being invented and the symbols still raw.

Nia Archives
Webster Hall, 125 East 11th Street, Manhattan · Fri, Nov 13

Nia Archives is doing something genuinely interesting — using jungle and drum & bass (UK rave subculture, heavily coded with community and identity) as a vehicle for personal storytelling; a small-room show before she outgrows rooms like this.

Surprise Chef
National Sawdust, 80 North 6th Street, Brooklyn · Fri, Nov 13

Australian instrumental group treating vintage soul and library music as systems to be decomposed and rebuilt — deeply craft-focused, zero hype, and National Sawdust is one of the best-designed listening rooms in New York.

artport: A History of Internet Art
Whitney Museum of American Art, 99 Gansevoort Street · Nov 21 – Mar 28

25 years of the Whitney's net art platform in one room — 100+ commissioned works from Golan Levin and Casey Reas to Morehshin Allahyari, with the gallery floor laid out like floating browser windows; this is the institutional history of the early internet as an artistic medium, made physical.
