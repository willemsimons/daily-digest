For you

FILM

Chantal Akerman

A film about invisible labor, ritual, and the brutal logic of systems hiding inside the domestic — the feedback loop between routine and selfhood made visible in real time. If you haven't sat with all 3h22m, now is the moment; if you have, it's one of those works that changes on every return.

ART
Black Sound / White Cube (and broader practice)
Theaster Gates

Gates builds institutions disguised as art — record archives, renovation projects, community land trusts — using the aesthetics of craft and Black music as both material and argument. His work lives exactly at the edge where art functions as community infrastructure, and where beauty is a form of political organizing.

MUSIC
Vespertine
Björk

A record built entirely around the domestic micro-scale — breath, heartbeat, the creak of ice — rendered in generative electronics, harpsichord, and a 50-voice choir. It's a system for turning interior states into weather. The physical/digital boundary doesn't exist here; it was dissolved in the studio.

Happening in New York

Sarah Michelson: nowhere
MoMA Atrium, 11 W 53rd St, Manhattan · Sep 15 – Oct 11

A live performance exhibition — not a show with a curtain but a durational, spatial presence running through the atrium over weeks — Michelson is one of the few choreographers who treats the architectural and social system of the gallery itself as the material.

Gaetano Pesce: A chair is a nest, is a foot, is a…
Salon 94, 1 Freeman Alley, Manhattan · Sep 17 – Oct 18

Pesce spent his career collapsing the boundary between object, body, and language — his furniture refuses to behave as furniture, which is the whole point; a tight gallery show of his work is a rare chance to be around objects that are genuinely trying to mean something.

Taryn Simon: Father Country I Do Love You
Solomon R. Guggenheim Museum, 1071 Fifth Ave, Manhattan · Sep 18 – Mar 14

Simon's first-ever fill of the entire Guggenheim rotunda — 42 interconnected works tracing one unnamed federal agent's life across photography, text, video, and sculpture, nearly a decade in the making; it's precisely about the invisible forces and hidden systems that shape national identity, and visitors choose their own path through the spiral.

Arthur Jafa: I Am Tony
The New Museum, 235 Bowery, Manhattan · Sep 24 – Jan 4

The largest Jafa survey ever mounted — two full floors of his filmic collage practice, including Love is the Message, The Message is Death and The White Album, plus new work; his method of cutting archival footage, music video, and documentary material into 'affective proximities' is exactly the kind of unreasonably deep system-building in image-making that rewards close attention.

The Armory Show 2026
Jacob K. Javits Convention Center, 429 11th Ave, Manhattan · Sep 25–27

230+ galleries across four days, with the curated Focus and Platform sections centering Caribbean diaspora artists — worth it less for the blue-chip booths than for the Presents section's first-timers and the nonprofit floor, where the fair's actual social logic becomes legible.
