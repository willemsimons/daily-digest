Biology doing things it wasn't supposed to

GLP-1s Beat Metformin for Prediabetes — 31% Lower T2D Risk, Fewer Heart Events, Longer Survival
American Heart Association

In 250,000+ adults with prediabetes and obesity, GLP-1 receptor agonists made participants 1.5× more likely to fully normalize blood sugar compared to metformin, with lower T2D progression, fewer heart attacks and strokes, and lower all-cause mortality over five years. This is the first large independent head-to-head comparison — not industry-funded — and it's already reshaping the prediabetes standard-of-care conversation. Still conference abstract, not yet peer-reviewed as a full manuscript.

Making things in space

Stringman: A $800 Cable-Driven Robot That Grips Across an Entire Room
Neufangled / Hacker News

Four motors, ceiling anchors, and a hanging gripper that covers any room up to 10m diagonal — with 1.9kg average payload versus 0.32kg for a comparable 7-actuator arm, because the load is shared across all lines simultaneously. At under $800 in materials with fully open-source 3D-printed parts, Stringman inverts the usual robotics cost model: instead of buying more actuators to reach further, you buy geometry. The clutter-immunity (it works from above, so floor chaos is irrelevant) is a legitimately useful design insight.

Off the map

Marian Wawrzeniecki's Neopagan Paintings: Archaeology as Myth, Myth as Political Act
Public Domain Review

A Polish painter-archaeologist who actually discovered the first obsidian artifacts on Polish soil used that fieldwork to construct an entirely parallel visual mythology — one designed to displace Christian iconography with a pre-Christian Slavic identity at a moment when Poland didn't legally exist as a country. The 1910 canvas described here, a skull-faced nude bisecting Art Nouveau tableaux with the title 'Old Truth Lies in Books,' sits at the uncomfortable intersection of serious scholarship and nationalist myth-fabrication. The Public Domain Review essay is one of those pieces that makes the word 'minor painter' feel like a category error.

Make something

Build a Stringman-style cable-position simulator in WebGL: four anchor points at ceiling corners, a hanging gripper, and real-time inverse kinematics as you drag the gripper through the volume. Visualize line tension as color — white at center where load is shared evenly, red near corners where one line takes the strain. Bonus: add a UI that lets you place obstacles on the floor and show the gripper navigating around them from above, the one thing wheeled robots can't do.
