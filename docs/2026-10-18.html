Gold Is Now America's Top Export — And Nobody Has an Official Explanation
Numismatic News

Since October 2025, gold has beaten oil, pharmaceuticals, and aircraft to become the highest-value US export almost every single month — and February 2026 may have set an all-time tonnage record. The flow is routing through Switzerland's refiners, then to Hong Kong (read: China), then London. No official explanation exists, but the signal is legible: foreign sovereigns are quietly swapping dollar reserves for metal, and the US mining and recycling base is the seller of last resort.

DIY Microfabrication Is Having Its RepRap Moment

RepRapMicron PIKA v0.02 — Open Source Sub-Micron 3D Printing
GitHub / RepRap Community

The RepRap community has printed a 0.5mm Benchy at 4μm layer height — and PIKA v0.02 is now achieving repeatable 1μm positioning within an 8mm cubic volume, depositing UV resin droplets as small as 4μm using a probe the machine etched itself. That last part is the tell: the tool making the tool. This is the exact trajectory FDM took in 2008, now aimed at MEMS-scale fabrication that currently requires cleanrooms.

Biology Doing Unexpected Things

Bleached Coral Recovers in Days — Just by Touching a Healthy Neighbor
Nature

A lab accident led to the discovery: Stephen Palumbi noticed two coral fragments accidentally in contact, and where bleached met healthy, color returned. Controlled experiments confirmed full reversal of bleaching within days of physical contact — the symbiotic algae (Symbiodiniaceae) appear to transfer through direct touch, though the exact signaling mechanism is still being worked out. The finding is preliminary and lab-scale, but it's the first time fast, full reversal has been demonstrated and it reframes what recovery might look like at the reef level.

How Things Get Made and Owned

Meta's New TOS Quietly Claims Scraping Rights Over Everything You Link To
Ars Technica

As of October 30, Meta's updated commercial terms will require any business account to grant Meta the right to crawl all domains and URLs associated with their products — no opt-out, just stop using Meta services if you disagree. Legal experts note this is structured to preempt the licensing battles news organizations are currently fighting with OpenAI: by getting consent baked into TOS, Meta sidesteps fair use entirely. If you have a studio website linked to an Instagram account, read this before the 30th.

Furifont — Pronunciation Built Into the Letterform Itself
antfu

Furigana — the small phonetic guides printed above Japanese kanji — normally require a separate annotation layer in HTML or typesetting. Furifont collapses this into the glyph itself: the pronunciation is part of the character, requiring no markup. It's a small, obsessive solution to a real problem, and it's worth five minutes of your time as a typographic idea about what a 'character' can contain.

Make something

Build a minimal XY plotter from two cheap steppers and a pen — not to draw, but to explore the gap between commanded position and actual position. Log the deviation at each point and render it as a field of error vectors. The map of where your machine lies is more interesting than anything it draws cleanly. This is the RepRapMicron problem at human scale: resolution vs. accuracy, and what the machine admits about itself.
